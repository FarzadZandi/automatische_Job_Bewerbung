# Method reference

Use one Claude chat and persistent files. First verify candidate truth into T1 evidence, T2 basic knowledge, and U unresolved claims. Then gather a current, deduplicated German-market corpus and normalize tasks, requirements, tools, methods, context, and outputs. Derive the smallest defensible number of role families: a family exists only when at least two of profile, evidence, competence order, project selection, search vocabulary, and letter proof selection materially change.

Test coverage, evidence density, market support, pairwise merges, splits, maintenance cost, and unseen-vacancy stability. Freeze contiguous B01–BN only after holdout validation. Build one German master CV, then truthful family baselines that preserve canonical employers and dates. Build German letter openings and T1 proof stories, with current employer facts deferred to application time. Derive keywords after families, separating profession, methods, tools, and context.

Use a fresh subagent as checker when available or a cold disk-based audit otherwise. Never invent evidence, promote learning plans, decide N in advance, cluster only by titles, create one CV per vacancy, or rely on chat history. Store exact generated outputs, selected baseline, evidence, risks, version, and status; package creation is not submission.

Versions: E evidence, R roles, M master, B baselines, C letters, K keywords. Increment affected layers after verified factual or taxonomy changes. Done when every claim has a source, families are distinct and market-supported, holdout confusion is acceptable, German documents are approved, runtime selects one family, and records are reproducible.
