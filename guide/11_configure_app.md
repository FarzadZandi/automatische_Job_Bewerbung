# Stage 11: configure the app

All answers are asked here; nothing is copied from anyone else's setup. Outputs: `config.yaml`, `Job App Docs/bewerberprofil.yaml`, `Job App Docs/antworten.yaml`, `companies.yaml`, `urls.txt`, `Job App Docs/Attachements/`.

1. **config.yaml** from `config.example.yaml`: contact_email; `keywords_path: "keywords.json"`; home_city and home_coordinates (look the coordinates up); `company_ats_location_terms` for the person's region; applicant_status (enrolled / graduating / graduated / professional) and graduation_date; `letter_writer: "claude"`, `claude_model: "haiku"`, `answer_language: "de"`; browser (auto / edge / chrome); `jd_archive_dir: "Bewerbungen/Stellenarchiv"`.
2. **Profile** (`profile.example.yaml` → `Job App Docs/bewerberprofil.yaml`), asked in boxes: salutation, academic title, first and last name, e-mail, phone, street, house number, postal code, city, country; optional birth date and nationality (may stay empty); LinkedIn, XING, GitHub, website; german_level and english_level from the intake; start_date; relocation, travel, driving licence; disability answer only if the person wants it stored (otherwise empty, the form stays manual). Propose `language_statement_de` (one sentence on German and English level, for ads that demand fluent German) and ask "Use this sentence?" (Ja / Ändern / Keinen Satz).
3. **Answer bank** (`answers.example.yaml` → `Job App Docs/antworten.yaml`), starting empty: facts (start date, salary expectation only if the person gives one, relocation, travel, driving licence, exact work-authorisation wording in the person's words, other languages); `tier2_skills` = all T2 tools from the ledger; for each T1 tool ask "How good are you with <tool>, 1 to 5?" and confirm the years proposed from the ledger. Notice period and sponsorship questions stay manual in forms.
4. **Company sources** (`companies.example.yaml` shows the format): 20 to 60 employers from the stage 3 corpus whose career pages use Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or SmartRecruiters; find their board names; check with `python3 scripts/validate_company_sources.py companies.yaml`; set `companies_enabled: true`.
5. **Arbeitsagentur searches** (`urls.txt`, format in `urls.example.txt`): one search URL per family with its main titles and the person's region and radius.
6. **Attachments:** ask which certificates go into one combined PDF (references, transcripts, degree, language certificates) and put it into `Job App Docs/Attachements/`; add its name to `application_attachment_allowlist`. Residence documents only if the person wants them used for explicit work-permit fields (then `application_residence_attachment_names`).
7. Copy the approved CVs, the paragraph bank and the letter template into `Job App Docs/` if not done yet. Run "Check setup" (double-click the check file, or the app button) until every line is OK.
